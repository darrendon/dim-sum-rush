Dim Sum Rush

A climbing game for 2–6 servers: teams of 2 or 3, or every server for themselves. Three rounds. Shown here with tiles; it plays the same with cards.

Learn to play

A coach walks you through the end of a round at a 4-player table, one move at a time. About 4 minutes.
