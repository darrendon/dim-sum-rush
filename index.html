Rules in brief

Your cart is your hand, in a fixed order you can never rearrange. The trolley is the face-up card in front of each player; you may use your own section's trolley cards in any combination, in any position. Combinations: single, set of 2–3, run of 2+. Cards from your cart must be side by side, and a run's cart cards must already be in rising or falling order.

Following: same kind and size with an equal or higher number, or a Chef's Special. Chef's Special: 4+ of a number or a run of 4+, all from your own cart. More cards beats fewer; at equal size a set beats a run; then an equal or higher number.

Passing takes you out of the trick. When you pass you may fill any empty spot on your side with a cart card, or swap a cart card with a trolley card (the taken card goes anywhere in your cart). Used trolley cards are not replaced.

Trick winner picks who in their section leads next. The round ends the moment every cart in one section is empty; the other section takes penalty points equal to its fullest cart. Three rounds, fewest points wins.

Every Server for Themselves: no sections and an open trolley. Using another player's trolley card tips them 1 point; your own is free. Fill only your own spot; swap with anyone's card. The trick winner leads; the round ends when any cart is empty and everyone else takes penalty points for their cart. Tips minus penalties, highest wins.
